Engineered Specifically for Assam's Ground Reality

Solving operational opacity, topographical flood challenges, and informal worker exclusion.

Universal Non-Invasive Scale Tap

Passive RS-232/RS-485 serial sniffer retrofitting to existing Avery and Essae weighbridges. Captures net weight in <3.5s with zero manual tampering or scale warranty voiding.

Flood-Aware Dynamic GIS Routing

Incorporates Assam State Disaster Management (ASDMA) flood inundation maps to divert PRIDE Bahan electric three-wheelers away from waterlogged corridors (Zoo Road, Anil Nagar), saving 24.8% in fleet fuel.

Vernacular FinTech & Direct DBT

Zero-text, icon-driven mobile PWA with Assamese & Bengali audio voice synthesis. Calculates fair price per kg and executes instant Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to collectors' bank accounts.

Cryptographic CPCB EPR Mints

Immutable mass-balance ledger converting physical material movements into tamper-proof SHA-256 QR-coded EPR certificates fully compliant with Central Pollution Control Board PWM guidelines.
